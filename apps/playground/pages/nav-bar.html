Nav Bar

A sticky glass navigation bar with scroll-linked shrink. Uses a CSS animation-timeline: scroll() at Crystalline+ and a JS scroll-listener fallback at Frosted/Matte. Shrink is structural (all tiers, including reduced-motion); depth cue (elevate/flatten) is suppressed under reduced motion. A shadow skip link targets #fluid-main-content.
