Visually Hidden

A CSS-only accessibility utility that clips content to 1×1 px — invisible to sighted users, fully present in the accessibility tree. No glass surface, no spring physics, no tier awareness. Its value in Fluid is pairing with glass and icon-heavy components that need accessible supplements without disrupting the visual design.

States

fluid-visually-hidden has one permanent state — always clipped. It has no hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, or error state of its own. Below: the consuming fluid-button carries state; the hidden label remains unchanged regardless.

Edge cases

No children — no visible or accessible content added:

(Element present in DOM but empty — no impact on layout or a11y tree) 

<!-- Empty element is safe — renders nothing -->
<fluid-visually-hidden></fluid-visually-hidden>

Long content — white-space:nowrap + overflow:hidden prevent the 1 px box from expanding:

This is an extremely long piece of text that would normally wrap across many lines and push layout elements apart, but white-space:nowrap and overflow:hidden keep the box at 1×1 px with no visible impact on surrounding layout. ← fluid-visually-hidden is here, 1×1 px (inspect to confirm) 
<fluid-visually-hidden>
  Very long text that would normally wrap…
</fluid-visually-hidden>
