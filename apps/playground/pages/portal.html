fluid-portal

Non-rendering host element that teleports its children to a <fluid-portal-root> at document.body. Inherits --fluid-* tokens from the nearest fluid-theme ancestor and allocates a z-index slot from ZIndexAllocator.

Variants

Default (overlay layer)

z-index allocated in the overlay range (100–499).

Overlay portal · bottom-right
 
<fluid-portal>
  <div>...</div>
</fluid-portal>

Sheet layer

layer="sheet" targets z-index 500–999.

Sheet portal · top-center
 
<fluid-portal layer="sheet">
  <div>...</div>
</fluid-portal>

System layer

layer="system" targets z-index ≥ 1000.

System portal · top bar
 
<fluid-portal layer="system">
  <div>...</div>
</fluid-portal>
